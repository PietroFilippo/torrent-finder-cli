# The Athanor paintings

Public-domain works, reduced to two tones by scripts/build_paintings.py from the
Wikimedia Commons scans below. The app recolours them in the chosen colourway.

- **The windmills**: Gustave Doré, 1863, *Don Quixote*. Public domain. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adventure_with_the_Windmills.jpg>

- **Charon**: Gustave Doré, 1861, *Inferno*. Public domain. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustave_Dor%C3%A9_-_Dante_Alighieri_-_Inferno_-_Plate_9_(Canto_III_-_Charon).jpg>
